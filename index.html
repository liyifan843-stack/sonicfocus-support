SonicFocus

Support

SonicFocus is a local-only ambient sound app for Mac. This page is the official support entry point.

Quick help

  • Choose a category, then tap a sound to add it to your mix.
  • Use the volume sliders to balance each sound layer.
  • Use the timer to stop playback automatically.
  • Menu Bar controls keep playback close at hand.
